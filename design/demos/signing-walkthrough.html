From draft to signed letter

How an intern offer letter moves through Flourishive: created by HR, approved, accepted by the candidate on their phone, signed with the HR Lead's DSC token, and checked by anyone through its QR code.

    Sample data only. The names, numbers and dates are made up, and the final look will follow the approved design system. In the real product the HR Lead's renewed Class 3 certificate is used; the current one has expired.
