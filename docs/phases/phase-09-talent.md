# Phase 9 — Talent

**Status:** Not started · **Depends on:** Phase 5 · **Detailed before the phase starts, with your approval**

## Goal

Hiring and performance run inside Flourishive and feed onboarding and letters directly.

## Scope

- [ ] Recruitment: job openings, a careers page per tenant, applications, pipeline stages, interview scheduling and feedback
- [ ] Offer creation flowing into Phase 2 letters and the Phase 5 onboarding checklist
- [ ] Performance: goals, review cycles, self and manager reviews, calibration
- [ ] Appraisal letters through the document engine and signing
- [ ] Training records and certificates

## Exit criteria

1. A candidate moves from application to signed offer to onboarding without leaving Flourishive.
2. One full review cycle completes with appraisal letters issued.
