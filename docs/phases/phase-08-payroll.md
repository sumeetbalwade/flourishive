# Phase 8 — Payroll

**Status:** Not started · **Depends on:** Phases 5 and 6 · **Detailed before the phase starts, with your approval**

## Goal

Monthly payroll for Flourisense in Maharashtra, with statutory deductions as dated, configurable rule sets rather than code, because the Labour Codes and state rules keep changing.

## Scope

- [ ] Salary structures and components; CTC breakdown templates per entity
- [ ] Rule sets as dated configuration: Code on Wages wage definition, PF, ESI, gratuity, leave encashment
- [ ] Maharashtra: Professional Tax slabs, Maharashtra Labour Welfare Fund
- [ ] TDS on salary: tax regime choice, investment declarations and proofs, Form 16
- [ ] Payroll run: inputs from attendance and leave, arrears, reimbursements, loss of pay, full-and-final settlement
- [ ] Payslips generated by the document engine and signed through the signing module
- [ ] Bank transfer files; statutory reports and returns data (PF ECR, ESI, PT)
- [ ] Payroll locking, approvals and audit

## Prerequisite

Rules must be confirmed with a chartered accountant or labour-law adviser before build.

## Exit criteria

1. Three parallel months of Flourisense payroll match the current payroll to the rupee.
2. Payslips are delivered signed, and statutory files validate.
